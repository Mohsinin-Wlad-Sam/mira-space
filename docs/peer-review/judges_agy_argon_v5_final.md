### 1. TECHNICAL (NASA Archive Software Engineer)

1. **Broken export & stale detector state in UI:** In [view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L784), the Export button uses `data-x="sources"`, which is not a valid `Tab` (`"changes"` is). Clicking it sets `tab = "sources"` ([view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L797)), falls through `renderPanel()` into the `else` branch (Astrometer), and downloads `astrometry.csv` instead of `field_changes.csv`. Furthermore, excluding flagged exposures (`again()` at [view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L768)) or toggling detectors ([view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L872)) never calls `runDetect()`, leaving the Motion Detector table and canvas boxes stale while single-source panels update.
2. **Numerical & resource edge cases:** In [detect.ts](../../web/src/blink/detect.ts#L75-L76), `fitLine` computes `dof = 2 * pts.length - 4`; for two-epoch links (e.g., WISE 2010 + one SPHEREx visit), `dof === 0`, setting `chi2 = 0` and `k = 1` with zero misfit penalty. In [view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L50), `median()` sorts without filtering `NaN`s (breaking JS comparator transitivity [VERIFIED]), and `fdb()` ([view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L57-L68)) opens a new IndexedDB connection on every read/write without closing it.
3. **Confusing visual & astrometric dual-models:** Pre-2025 playback in `Evolution` mode ([view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L342)) displays the 2025 SPHEREx image dimmed to 55% while moving the overlay box to its 1999/2010 position—visually detaching the marker from the star on the canvas. Meanwhile, the Astrometer tab ([view.ts](../../web/src/blink/view.ts#L698-L699)) shows two different proper-motion fits (Gaia-registered visit stacks in `detect.ts` vs. uncalibrated per-exposure centroids in `answers.ts`) that can disagree.
4. **Assistant proxy security:** In [assistant.mjs](../../web/server/assistant.mjs#L62), rate-limiting extracts `req.headers["x-forwarded-for"].split(",")[0]`. Because the leftmost `X-Forwarded-For` entry is client-controlled unless stripped by a trusted reverse proxy [VERIFIED], an attacker can spoof IPs on every POST to bypass the 40/10-min limit and exhaust the 4,000/day global quota (`MIRA_LLM_DAILY`). The endpoint also lacks `Origin` checks and accepts up to 6 client-driven tool rounds per turn ([assistant.ts](../../web/src/assistant.ts#L81)).

### 2. SPACE SCIENCE (Infrared Astronomer — Brown Dwarfs / High Proper Motion)

1. **Circularity in the 364-field control validation:** The headline claims in [README.md](../../README.md#L40-L43), [docs/15_product_brief.md](../../docs/15_product_brief.md#L61-L64), and [docs/10_submission_draft.md](../../docs/10_submission_draft.md#L42-L43)—that 170/173 known Gaia movers are recovered (98%) and 0/4,665 stationary Gaia stars are flagged—are not blind tests. First, in [detect.ts](../../web/src/blink/detect.ts#L209-L212), when a source has a Gaia parallax/motion (`hasPm(g)`), `back(yr)` uses Gaia's own catalogued proper motion to project backward to 2010.5 and 1999.5 to find its AllWISE and 2MASS counterparts, whereas [validate_detector.mts](../../web/scripts/validate_detector.mts#L24-L26) passes `cat.gaia` unmasked. Cold T and Y dwarfs are optically faint beyond Gaia's $G \approx 21$ mag limit [VERIFIED] and must rely on the blind `orphanW` linker ([detect.ts](../../web/src/blink/detect.ts#L270-L296)). Second, in [detect.ts](../../web/src/blink/detect.ts#L304-L306), any Gaia star whose fitted motion disagrees with Gaia by $\ge \max(4\sigma, 0.04''/\text{yr})$ is tagged `BLENDED` instead of `MOVED`. Thus, stationary Gaia stars (`pm < 0.04''/yr` in [validate_detector.mts](../../web/scripts/validate_detector.mts#L31)) are prevented from becoming `MOVED` false alarms (`pm >= 0.08''/yr`, `sig >= 5`) by Gaia's own prior.
2. **Under-qualified methane classification:** In [verify_merge.py](../../census/verify_merge.py#L97-L99) and [verify_merge.py](../../census/verify_merge.py#L161-L162), "methane dwarf" is assigned whenever `REF > 5 * eREF`, `CH4 / REF < 0.6`, and `W2 > W1`, without bounding the error `eCH4` at 3.3 µm or accounting for 3.3 µm PAH/ice features and undersampled LVF phase scatter across 6.15″ pixels [VERIFIED].
3. **Funnel presentation & referee question:** Dropping the unverified brightening/vanishing lists and hiding $|b| < 10^\circ$ ([catalogue.ts](../../web/src/catalogue.ts#L35-L52)) is scientifically sound (though [docs/15_product_brief.md](../../docs/15_product_brief.md#L69) says `202,548` re-measured vs. `202,528` in [README.md](../../README.md#L65), and Slide 5 in [docs/10_submission_draft.md](../../docs/10_submission_draft.md#L85) cites 81% Gaia match vs. 88% in the text). A referee will immediately ask: *"What are the completeness and false-alarm rates on the 364 control fields when Gaia proper motions are masked from `detectChanges`?"*

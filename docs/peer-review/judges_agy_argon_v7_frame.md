### 2. A SPACE SCIENTIST

1. **Ephemeris and lunar formulas ([ephem.ts](../../web/src/ephem.ts#L27-L66) `helio`, `bodiesAt`):** `helio(2, yr)` uses Standish's Earth–Moon barycentre elements as the geocenter, and `bodiesAt()` omits light-time ($t - \Delta/c$) and annual aberration ($\sim 20.5''$). For the Moon, `bodiesAt()` subtracts J2000 longitude precession (`- 1.397 * T`, L52) without rotating ecliptic latitude `mb` for the obliquity rate ($\sim 47''/\text{cy}$), omits topocentric diurnal parallax (up to $\sim 1^\circ$ error on the local sky), and omits $5\log_{10}(r\Delta)$ from `mag` (L56), yielding `mag = -3.8` at New Moon (`mpsi = 0`) so `meetings()` ([skymap.ts](../../web/src/skymap.ts#L199-L203)) announces daytime New Moon conjunctions as bright events. For Saturn (`k === 5`, L62), ring tilt `sB` mixes J2000 `lon, lat` with an epoch-of-date node rate (`- 1.395 * T`).
2. **Horizon and rise-time maths ([skymap.ts](../../web/src/skymap.ts#L160-L182) `lstAt`, `altAz`, `myView`, `horizon`):** `altAz()` subtracts ICRS/J2000 `ra` directly from apparent GMST (`lstAt(yr)`), ignoring ~26 years of precession ($\sim 22'$ in RA). `myView()` and `horizon()` test geometric `alt > 0` instead of the standard $-0^\circ 50'$ refraction/semi-diameter horizon, and `myView()` steps 24 hours (`m = 5..1440`, L168) holding `b.ra, b.dec` constant—making moonrise ~50 minutes early because the Moon moves ~13° eastward per day.
3. **SPHEREx coverage and mover lighting ([skymap.ts](../../web/src/skymap.ts#L112-L125) `reveal`, `draw` L252–260; [sphx_coverage.py](../../census/sphx_coverage.py#L30-L36); [sphx_index2.py](../../census/sphx_index2.py#L36-L53) `pick_spread`):** `pick_spread()` keeps only the midpoint when `q == 1` (L51), collapsing multi-day visits in `coverage.json` to zero length. `reveal()` then fakes a 2–14 day ramp (`L115`), blurs nside-32 cells on a non-area-preserving $(l,b)$ grid, counts visits as complete on their *first* day (`day >= v[q]`, L116), and reveals a full-survey co-added JPEG. In `draw()` (L258), mover dots never move with time and light up at `day >= firstEnd[CELL[k]]`—after the cell's *first* SPHEREx visit, when a single pass cannot confirm SPHEREx proper motion.
4. **Multi-band co-adding and zodiac cuts ([placeview.ts](../../web/src/placeview.ts#L49-L63) `loadSpherex`; [ephem.ts](../../web/src/ephem.ts#L68-L70) `zodiac`):** `loadSpherex()` pulls `maxFrames: 12` across all 6 detectors (0.75–5 µm) without Gaia registration or flux scaling, then runs `robustStack()` ([imaging.ts](../../web/src/blink/imaging.ts#L25-L34)), whose top-20% outlier trimmer discards the 4–5 µm exposures where cold brown dwarfs shine. Finally, `zodiac()` uses 1D ecliptic-longitude cuts instead of Delporte's tilted B1875 RA/Dec boundaries and returns `""` whenever `|b.lat| > 4°` (blanking the Moon, Mercury, and Venus near nodes/conjunctions).

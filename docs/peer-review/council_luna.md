## 1. SCIENTIFIC ACCURACY

1. **Proper motion is treated as constant angular motion even when distance and radial velocity matter.** `sphxlive.ts` propagates RA/Dec with a tangent-plane approximation and no perspective acceleration ([sphxlive.ts:43](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:43)); the app itself notes the approximation for Barnard’s Star ([sky.ts:201](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sky.ts:201)). That is fine over the WISE–SPHEREx baseline, but “every SPHEREx pass” can span years and the app also offers much longer forecasts. State the epoch and propagation limits wherever results are shown.

2. **The live flipbook’s wavelength and flux interpretation needs calibration evidence.** It assigns wavelengths from a detector lookup and reports aperture flux using a fixed 6.15″ pixel area and no aperture correction ([sphxlive.ts:125](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:125), [sphxlive.ts:135](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:135)). It subtracts the stamp median as background ([sphxlive.ts:122](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:122)), which may include source flux for crowded or extended fields. Call the spectrum an approximate, uncalibrated diagnostic unless validated against IRSA photometry.

3. **The full-frame read assumes simple FITS image layout.** The code treats the second HDU as the science image and reads rows as contiguous big-endian float32 ([sphxlive.ts:111](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:111), [sphxlive.ts:115](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:115), [sphxlive.ts:119](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:119)). It does not account for `BITPIX`, `BSCALE`/`BZERO`, or compressed/table HDUs. Document and validate the supported products, or the direct archive measurements can silently be wrong.

4. **The CME “Earth-bound” classification is only a cone-direction test.** It compares angular separation with the half-angle ([live.ts:119](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/live.ts:119)), then labels the CME “Earth-bound” in the scene ([space.ts:815](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/space.ts:815)). Cone overlap is not an arrival prediction; it ignores CME width/model uncertainty and propagation to Earth. Label it “cone points toward Earth” or omit the impact claim.

5. **The story makes unsupported physical and pipeline claims.** “Only cold worlds … have methane” and “exactly as in Jupiter’s atmosphere” are too categorical ([main.ts:486](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:486), [main.ts:614](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:614)). “Our AI and the 16-year motions flagged …” should distinguish candidate selection from confirmation ([main.ts:618](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:618)). Likewise, claims that the dots follow measured motion “exactly” oversell the catalog’s precision ([main.ts:612](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:612)).

6. **The WISE–SPHEREx slider implies a continuous image timeline that does not exist.** The source images are fixed to WISE 2010.5 or SPHEREx 2025.84, with a crossfade between them ([sky.ts:234](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sky.ts:234)); the UI calls this a “time machine” and plays moving dots over it ([main.ts:611](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:611)). Make the crossfade/epoch distinction explicit so users do not mistake interpolated display for observations at intermediate dates.

7. **The 3D velocity and brightness presentation needs stronger caveats.** The 3D view says objects move on measured 3D velocities and brightness is physical ([space.ts:1](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/space.ts:1)); only some catalog objects have radial velocities, and AI distances are inferred. The view should visibly distinguish measured from inferred distances and mark missing radial velocities rather than implying uniformly measured 3D trajectories.

## 2. USER EXPERIENCE

1. **The opening view is overloaded.** Six “lenses,” live Solar System and horizon toggles, a survey ladder, time slider, color control, and several top-level modes compete for attention ([main.ts:54](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:54), [main.ts:65](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:65), [main.ts:76](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:76), [main.ts:82](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:82)). A first-time visitor needs one obvious “inspect a target” path and a compact explanation of what is observed versus overlaid.

2. **The core coordinate workflow is hidden behind right-click or long press.** The prompt says enter RA/Dec, but map inspection depends on context-menu gestures ([main.ts:137](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:137), [main.ts:143](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:143)). The search box placeholder is vague, and there is no obvious coordinate form or “inspect here” button for touch users. Add a visible coordinate entry action.

3. **The results panel mixes evidence, interpretation, and exploration.** Dossier panels combine motion, AI claims, flipbook, spectrum, light curve, and 3D actions ([main.ts:458](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:458), [main.ts:499](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:499), [main.ts:505](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:505)). Judges may not know which number or plot supports the headline. Put provenance, units, and confidence beside each claim and keep a short summary above the advanced views.

4. **Some wording risks misleading non-specialists.** “Census live” sounds like a live sky survey, while the census is a precomputed catalog; “My sky now” and “Solar System now” are genuinely live features ([main.ts:62](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:62), [main.ts:69](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:69)). Rename the census status to indicate catalog/data refresh time.

5. **The long guided tour is the main onboarding, but it is dense and sequential.** It includes several specialist targets and claims before reaching “Your turn” ([main.ts:611](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:611)). Add skip/quick-start affordance and let users jump directly to the mandatory targets.

6. **Mobile interactions are fragile.** The UI requires small hit targets across several control bars, and mobile map inspection relies on a 650 ms long press that can conflict with map gestures ([main.ts:143](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:143)). Expose coordinate inspection through a visible button or tap mode; keep essential controls reachable when the bottom panel is open.

7. **The live flipbook can fail without a helpful recovery path.** It depends on the exposure index and many direct range requests, but the user-facing spot view presents it as built live from NASA’s archive ([main.ts:570](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:570), [sphxlive.ts:97](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:97)). Surface “no coverage,” “archive unavailable,” and “some frames failed” states with links to IRSA.

## 3. CHALLENGE FIT

1. **Strong core alignment:** arbitrary RA/Dec lookup, SPHEREx exposure selection, time-ordered stamps, and detector/LVF wavelength display address the challenge’s central “little video” request ([main.ts:558](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:558), [sphxlive.ts:1](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:1), [sphxlive.ts:93](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/sphxlive.ts:93)).

2. **The mandatory targets are present in the guided narrative, but their test criteria are unevenly demonstrated.** Barnard, Epsilon Indi B, WISE 0855, RW Aur, and R Dra are tour stops ([main.ts:612](/Users/abdelmonaimbounite/nasa-space-apps/assrar-dawla/web/src/main.ts:612)); however, the tour does not visibly prove Barnard’s saturation/overflow handling, Epsilon Indi’s 6.7′ common motion/FOV choice, WISE 0855’s lack of short-band signal, or RW Aur/R Dra cadence counts.

3. **The app’s strongest challenge feature is buried.** Live flipbooks are central, yet the quickest route is right-click/long press, while the screen foregrounds census lenses and 3D features. Make “Enter RA/Dec → Build SPHEREx flipbook” the primary first-screen action.

4. **Potential considerations are only partially surfaced.** The app has a spectrum and URL copy, but the visible challenge workflow lacks a clear FOV/data-volume choice, image subtraction, contamination flags, and explicit overlay controls for Gaia/WISE. The static census and live SPHEREx cutout are separate experiences.

5. **The promised audience story is diffuse.** 3D Space, AI candidate discovery, Solar System live data, and Lab compete with the challenge’s focused SPHEREx time-domain use case. Judges need a short path from challenge problem to actual SPHEREx frames, with sources and validation alongside the frames.

6. **Single best improvement for the remaining ~24 hours:** make a polished, one-screen **“Try the five challenge targets”** launcher that opens each target’s live SPHEREx flipbook directly, with per-target proof points (measured cadence/exposure count, motion rate, relevant bands, saturation/coverage status) and one compact provenance/limitations note. It turns the existing implementation into an immediately judgeable demonstration and exposes the criteria the current tour leaves implicit.

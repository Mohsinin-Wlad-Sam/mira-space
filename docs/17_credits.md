| The opening (0 to 9.5 s) | The real stars around Epsilon Indi B: positions, motions, brightness and colours of 838 stars from ESA Gaia DR3 (CDS VizieR I/355/gaiadr3), drawn by the film's own code as a photograph would show them. The six panes of glass stand for SPHEREx's six detectors; under each, the object is as bright as SPHEREx measured it in that band (the Lab's own median fluxes: 1.20, 25.0, 13.7, 8.98, 50.8, 18.7 mJy). `web/public/film/field.json` | Data: ESA Gaia DR3 (CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO), 2MASS. The drawing is the team's own and is named as such on the closing card. Not generated by an AI model |
| The Lab (9.5 to 22 s) | The running site in a frame: NASA's real pictures of the field (2MASS 1999, WISE 2010, SPHEREx 2025 to 2026), the Lab's own time bar, tools and readings | Real data; every figure on screen is the instrument's own |
| The whole sky (22 to 26 s) | MIRA's SPHEREx panorama, made from NASA's SPHEREx all-sky cube, in Mollweide projection (galactic coordinates), with the 17,604 confirmed movers at their real places, each drawn along its measured motion, then the 635 that neither Gaia nor SIMBAD lists | Data: NASA/JPL-Caltech, NASA/IPAC IRSA; the team's measurements on NASA SPHEREx exposures |

No picture in the film is AI-generated, and the film no longer shows the Sky model's forecast (the one picture a
network draws in the product, which carries its own "AI-generated · prediction" label in the Lab).

**Why no commercial music or film footage.** The Space Apps submission guide says: "If you are using any copyrighted
materials, make sure you have permission to use them." A credit line is not permission, and platforms mute or
block videos that carry commercial tracks. The film therefore uses only material whose licence already grants the
use: a Creative Commons track and the site's own pictures of NASA data.

## Models and AI tools

The assistant runs on K2 Horizon (Institute of Foundation Models). The sky world model was trained by the team
(`census/wm_train.py`). Every use of an AI tool in building MIRA is logged in `docs/07_si_usage_log.md`.
